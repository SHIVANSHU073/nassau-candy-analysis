# Limitations

- Recorded order-to-ship intervals are implausibly long and require source validation.
- Product/factory effects are not identifiable from the deterministic mapping.
- Factory capacity is unavailable.
- Transportation/reassignment cost is unavailable.
- Production lead time and inventory constraints are unavailable.
- Customer coordinates are unavailable.
- Scenario outputs are counterfactual model proxies.
- Predictive performance on the supplied target is weak.
